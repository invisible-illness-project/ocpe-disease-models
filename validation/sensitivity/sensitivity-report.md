## Interpretation limits

- hEDS lower-limb venous compliance includes the healthy reference value and exploratory changes; it is not a measured hEDS coefficient.
- ME/CFS delay and onset constants are phenomenological and are not probability distributions over patients.
- CO2 reactivity, clipping, and cerebral-outflow scenarios vary explicit computational assumptions; they do not identify patient-specific reactivity or convert ETCO2 into PaCO2.
- Configuration hashes and solver settings are retained in JSON; no random seed is used because no stochastic sampling occurs.
- Sensitivity does not validate equation structure or clinical utility.
